# Molekule air-purifier candidate

## Identification and use

The `MOLEKULE…` name prefix suggests a Molekule connected air purifier, but
does not identify a model. The manufacturer's Molekule Air setup guide documents
Bluetooth pairing followed by selection of a Wi-Fi network. That is a
documented setup capability, **not** a verified session of the candidate.

Molekule also publishes a separate list of models cleared for medical use.
A brand-name discovery does **not** establish one of those models, FDA clearance
of the candidate, therapeutic effectiveness, a hospital installation, or
ownership. No raw ADV/GATT or purifier operation was verified.
