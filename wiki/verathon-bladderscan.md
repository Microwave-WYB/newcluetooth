# Verathon BladderScan family candidate

## Weak identification lead

The research name `BladderSc` is consistent with Verathon's BladderScan
bladder-volume ultrasound family, but **no manufacturer-documented exact
Bluetooth-name fingerprint was found**. Neither a Verathon identity nor the
BVI 9400 model is confirmed. This optional entry retains a low-confidence lead
rather than presenting an identified clinical instrument.

## What the reference manuals establish

For the **reference BVI 9400**, the probe is wired to the console. Its documented
Bluetooth route is console → battery-charger/wireless hub → USB-connected PC
running ScanPoint/QuickPrint. Manuals describe saved annotated-exam uploads,
calibration workflows, and instrument/console software updates; ultrasound
scanning itself does not require the optional PC software. These are reference
product functions, not capabilities demonstrated for the research candidate.

The related FCC index for ScanPoint Remote is not a verified FCC ID of the
observed signal. No raw ADV/GATT, patient-data exposure, pairing weakness,
update-signing property, vulnerability, hospital deployment, or ownership was
established.
