# Unassigned name prefixes: no product identification

## How to recognize it

- **Local name:** `DT-TAG*`, `Envoy*`, `FSCE*`, `FJLB*`, `FSLB*`, `FCBB*`, `FCGE*`, `FJTA*`, `TNNOW`, `raven*`, `memo-dem*`, `TCL_B*`, `quec_ble_*`, `gate*`, `rnet`, `HN_ACCF*`, `glow`, `sande`, `Orange`, `FI-FC*`, `DEI-*`, `BTSQ*`, `Tsunghanrog`, `AZPDKC*`, `OMNI`, `LDL-*`, `XH_CL_B*`, `PROV_*`, `DFU-V*`, `office*`, `Baxter*`, `IOTWF*`, `LMU_*`, `Venus_*`, `WLLINK-*`, `BLE_*`, `Fixture *`
- **Company field:** company field 881, company field 256, company field 1087, company field 23040, company field 23041, company field 23042, company field 9474, company field 65024, company field 1468
- **Service / service-data UUID:** `FFF1`, `FE59`, `FE2C`, `1800`, `6666`
- **AD shape (ordered type/data length):** `01/1,FF/6,09/13,FF/14`, `01/1,FF/25,0A/1,03/2,16/13,09/5`, `01/1,03/2,19/2,FF/8,09/8`, `01/1,FF/9,09/12`
- **What it is:** Name prefixes, shapes and company fields examined and left without a reliable product, vendor or category.
- **Confidence and limits:** No product identification; every entry lists why it remains unassigned. Identifiers restate values recorded in the entries below; shared or generic values are flagged there.

These are **product or family candidates**, not authenticated device identities. A fingerprint may combine a sanitized local name, advertised UUID, manufacturer field or ordered AD structure; **shape need not be unique**. Counts below are exact `(addr,raw)` advertisement variants / distinct observed addresses / summed scans from read-only queries of simi's first-party `cluetooth` database on **2026-10-16**. These are different from physical-device counts. Research date is not observation date. No hospital relationship is required or inferred. AD lengths below count **data bytes only**, excluding the length and type bytes; types and lengths preserve their observed order.

## Unassigned name groups

`DT-TAG*` occurs in **18 variants / 17 addresses / 279 scans** with ordered types `01, FF, 09, 12, 0A`. Seventeen variants have corresponding data lengths `1,25,15,4,1` and iBeacon-format manufacturer data; one has `1,16,15,4,1` and a different auxiliary company value. A name field has a trailing NUL. No reliable matching product/model documentation was found. **Do not attribute it to a tag vendor or UWB technology** from the name or iBeacon frame alone.

`Envoy*` has **9 variants / 9 addresses / 72 scans**, with no company field: eight advertise generic `1800` and one generic Espressif provisioning UUID `021a9004-0382-4aea-bff4-6b3f1c5adfb4`. An [Enphase Envoy-S gateway](https://investor.enphase.com/news-releases/news-release-details/enphase-energy-launches-next-generation-envoy-s) exists, but no evidence binds these broadcasts to Enphase; brand/model remain **unassigned**.

Unassigned name groups share parsed company field 881 and chiefly `01/1, FF/6, 09/13, FF/14` (a few variants omit the final `FF`): `FSCE*` (**462 variants / 462 addresses / 1,008 scans**), `FJLB*` (**307 / 307 / 679**), and `FSLB*` (**226 / 226 / 446**). A 2026-10-19 read-only follow-up found the same 13-byte-name shape on further four-letter prefixes: `FCBB*` (**90 / 90 / 165**), `FCGE*` (**49 / 49 / 113**), `FJTA*` (**41 / 41 / 52**), `CCGB*` (**8 / 8 / 9**), `FCEB*` (**5 / 5 / 12**), `EWAB*` (**4 / 4 / 4**) and `FCAB*` (**3 / 3 / 7**). All company-field-881 advertisements together are **1,223 variants / 1,223 addresses / 2,585 scans**, and 22 distinct four-letter prefixes occur among the 13-byte names; every variant has its own address and name, so the family looks like one name-per-device scheme. Their suffixes stay private. The [SIG Assigned Numbers HTML dated 2023-12-27](https://www.bluetooth.com/wp-content/uploads/Files/Specification/HTML/Assigned_Numbers/out/en/index-en.html) lists **881 / `0x0371` as ORBCOMM**, while the [official current company-identifiers YAML](https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/raw/main/assigned_numbers/company_identifiers/company_identifiers.yaml) checked in this follow-up omitted that number; no revocation or reassignment is inferred. No manufacturer maps these names to a broadcast product. Neither the old allocation, shared shape nor field proves one maker, product class, three models, or a relationship among these groups.

An additional **11 variants / 11 addresses / 151 scans** in a custom-name cohort have service-data UUID `FE2C`, the [Google Fast Pair service](https://developers.google.com/nearby/fast-pair/specifications/service/provider), but **no confirmed product manufacturer/model**. The possibly personal name is not published or used to combine these with the separate Bose candidate.

`TNNOW` (case-insensitive exact five-byte name) has **34 variants / 33 addresses / 600 scans**, all advertising service/service-data UUID `FFF1` with `01/1,FF/25,0A/1,03/2,16/13,09/5`. Company field 76 is not brand proof. An [independent conference-badge teardown](https://xkyle.com/reInvent-2023-Badge-Teardown/) mentions a similarly named BLE module, but its `FFF1` is a **GATT characteristic**, not proof of matching advertising protocol. Manufacturer, model and conference deployment remain **unassigned**.

[Raven Connected's vehicle-camera/telematics product](https://ravenconnected.com/product/) is only a same-name possibility; no public match binds these broadcasts to the vendor, so **manufacturer and category remain unassigned**.

Although [MEMO sells BLE-connected display locks](https://memolocks.com/en/order-platform/product/air2-line-network-starter-package/), no source maps this broadcast name to a MEMO product; the two assigned company fields do **not** support that attribution. **Manufacturer, use and model remain unknown.**

A 2026-10-21 read-only follow-up recorded four more case-insensitive name prefixes that no public source we found explains. Counts are exact `(addr,raw)` variants / distinct addresses / scans from simi's first-party `cluetooth` database; the shapes below list AD types in observed order (data lengths were not re-measured for them).

`IOTWF*` (observed upper case, 8-byte name: the prefix plus three hexadecimal-looking characters, suffix hidden) has **16 exact variants / 16 addresses / 722 scans**, ordered `01,16,09,FF` (13 variants) or `FF,16,01,09` (3 variants), no advertised service UUID. Its service-data UUID is `5A00`, `5A01` or `5A02` (**3 / 3 / 29**, **1 / 1 / 1**, **12 / 12 / 692**), and the parsed company field is 23040, 23041 or 23042, and the variant counts per value match the service-data counts, so the two fields appear to carry the same `5A0x` value. Neither 23040-23042 is listed in the [official company-identifiers YAML](https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/raw/main/assigned_numbers/company_identifiers/company_identifiers.yaml), and a generic web search for the name found nothing. The name suffix equals the end of the address in only one of 16 variants, so it is not a plain address tail. Manufacturer, category and what `5A0x` encodes remain **unknown**.

`LMU_*` (observed `LMU_` plus ten digits, 14-byte name) has **22 exact variants / 22 addresses / 249 scans**, ordered `01,06,09,12,0A`, no company field, with one shared 128-bit service UUID (withheld). The digits do not equal the address tail. No public source was found; manufacturer and use are **unknown**.

`Venus_*` (observed `Venus_` plus twelve hexadecimal characters, 18-byte name) has **35 exact variants / 35 addresses / 1,014 scans**, ordered `01,06,FF,0A,09`, one shared 128-bit service UUID (withheld) and a single parsed company field 9474, which is not assigned in the YAML. In all 35 variants the twelve-character suffix equals the advertising address written without separators, so the name is derived from the address and one variant is one name. No public source was found for the name, and the shared UUID has no known public binding. Maker and category remain **unknown**.

`WLLINK-*` (observed upper case, `WLLINK-` plus four hexadecimal-looking characters, 11-byte name) has **8 exact variants / 8 addresses / 297 scans**, ordered `09,FF`, company field 65024 (`0xFE00`, not assigned in the YAML), no flags field and no service or service-data UUID. The suffix does not equal the address tail in any variant. A web search found only unrelated Texas Instruments WiLink chip pages, which are **not** evidence for this name. Manufacturer and category remain **unknown**.

A 2026-10-22 read-only follow-up found two more small name cohorts with no public source we could tie to them. Counts are exact `(addr,raw)` variants / distinct addresses / scans from simi's first-party `cluetooth` database, excluding company field 76.

`BLE_*` (literal underscore, `BLE_` plus four upper-case hexadecimal characters, 8-byte name) has **29 exact variants / 29 addresses / 234 scans**, all ordered `01/1,03/2,19/2,FF/8,09/8`. Every variant advertises 16-bit service UUID `6666`, GAP Appearance 2000 (`0x07D0`) and a parsed company field 0; the four-character suffix equals the end of the advertising address in all 29, so the name is address-derived. The [SIG Assigned Numbers appearance list](https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/raw/main/assigned_numbers/core/appearance_values.yaml) puts 2000 in category `0x01F` **Light Source**, but subcategory `0x10` is not defined there; and `6666` is in neither the [SIG service-UUID list](https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/raw/main/assigned_numbers/uuids/service_uuids.yaml) nor the member-UUID list. Searches for the name, UUID and shape found no public product documentation. The appearance is a self-declared category hint only; **maker, product and whether it is a lamp, controller or module remain unknown**. `6666` appears in 31 variants / 31 addresses / 269 scans overall, the other 2 variants do not match the `BLE_` name form and are not combined with this cohort.

`Fixture *` (observed `Fixture ` with a space plus four upper-case hexadecimal characters, 12-byte name, suffix hidden) has **14 exact variants / 14 addresses / 77 scans**, all ordered `01/1,FF/9,09/12` with no advertised service UUID and parsed company field 1468. The [SIG company-identifier list](https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/raw/main/assigned_numbers/company_identifiers/company_identifiers.yaml) assigns 1468 (`0x05BC`) to Leviton Mfg. Co., Inc., and these 14 are the **only** company-1468 advertisements in the database. The suffix is not the address tail and every variant has its own random-looking address prefix. [Leviton's community](https://communities.leviton.com/threads/whats-the-qr-code-for-on-levitons-devices.88) mentions Bluetooth discovery for newer Intellect modules, but no source found documents this local name or manufacturer-data layout. A company field is not authentication of the product: **a Leviton association is a candidate only; product line, role (fixture, module, sensor) and model remain unknown.** Generic English `Fixture` is not evidence of a lighting luminaire.
