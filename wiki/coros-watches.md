# COROS `COROS*` GPS sports watches

## How to recognize it

- **Local name:** `COROS*`
- **Service / service-data UUID:** `180A`, `180F`, `FEE7`
- **What it is:** COROS PACE/APEX/VERTIX watches distinguished by model-name token.
- **Confidence and limits:** Model-name candidates; shared services are not model identifiers. Identifiers restate values recorded in the entries below; shared or generic values are flagged there.

Read-only simi `cluetooth` aggregates researched **2026-10-16**. The names below support **product/model candidates**, not authenticated identities. Counts are exact `(addr,raw)` variants / distinct observed addresses / scans, not physical devices. Hide any device-specific suffixes; research date is not observation date.
